# Design Direction: Renaldy Imran Hermawan Portfolio
**Role:** Web3 & Decentralized Infrastructure Engineer | Cloud & DevOps Specialist  
**Dial:** ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1  

---

## 1. Identity & Persona
- **Audience:** Web3 protocol leads, engineering managers, DevOps recruiters, blockchain infra architects, and node operator teams.
- **Mood:** High-assurance, cryptographic precision, reliable telemetry, and decentralized resilience (zero generic AI slop, zero useless 3D coin bloat).
- **Core Motif:** Live node observability, verified contract pipelines, cryptographic hash metadata pills, and terminal-grade interface clarity.

---

## 2. Dials (Antislop Standard)
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)**  
  *Rationale:* Balances deep cypherpunk tech gravitas (obsidian base `#0A0D14`, cryptographic emerald `#10B981`, and telemetry cyan `#06B6D4`) with human clarity and readability.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Structured with Intentional Variety)**  
  *Rationale:* Alternates between live on-chain status telemetry, narrative career context, interactive pipeline simulators, and timeline audit logs—avoiding monotonous card grids.
- **MOTION: 1 (Calm, Purposeful & Functional)**  
  *Rationale:* Subtle state transitions, ping indicators on healthy RPC nodes, and live streaming console logs. No distracting parallax, ambient floating blobs, or gratuitous spinning 3D coins.

---

## 3. Typography Stack & Rationale
- **Headings: `Outfit` (sans-serif, weights 600, 700, 800)**  
  *Rationale:* Clean geometric structure giving punchy modern authority to protocol metrics and engineering headlines.
- **Body: `Inter` (sans-serif, weights 400, 500, 600)**  
  *Rationale:* High legibility at reading densities across deep technical architecture explanations and CV documentation.
- **Code / Monospace: `JetBrains Mono` (monospace, weights 400, 500)**  
  *Rationale:* Crucial for cryptographic addresses (`0x...`), transaction hashes, gas metrics, terminal simulator output, and Solidity/YAML snippets.

---

## 4. Color Palette & One-Line Rationales (R-31)
- **Background Obsidian (`#0A0D14` dark, `#F8FAFC` light):**  
  *Rationale:* Deep non-straining background maximizing contrast for telemetry data while maintaining WCAG AA ratio >= 7:1.
- **Cryptographic Emerald (`#10B981` / Dark: `#34D399`):**  
  *Rationale:* Visual indicator of healthy blockchain consensus, verified smart contracts, and synchronized nodes.
- **Telemetry Cyan (`#06B6D4` / Dark: `#22D3EE`):**  
  *Rationale:* Represents real-time RPC connectivity, peer latency, and observability data streams (Grafana/Prometheus).
- **Primary Violet/Indigo (`#6366F1` / Dark: `#818CF8`):**  
  *Rationale:* Grounded protocol accent for active controls and links without neon oversaturation.
- **Surface Layer (`#111827` / Border `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`):**  
  *Rationale:* Clean elevation boundary for content modules without relying on blurry pseudo-3D drop shadows.

---

## 5. Architectural & Interaction Guidelines
- **Focus Rings:** Distinct 2px `:focus-visible` ring in accent cyan (`#06B6D4`) for keyboard accessibility (WCAG 2.4.7).
- **Dose Caps:** Glow is strictly capped at active state dots (e.g. green pulse for synchronized validator node); never sprayed on cards or static containers.
- **Web3 Telemetry Bar:** Displays real-time on-chain cluster health (Ethereum block height, gas in Gwei, Solana TPS, and ENS identity).
